Privacy

Last updated November 6, 2026

The short version
  • Henwood has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and never sells data.
  • Your pantry, recipes, lists and chats stay on your device and in your own iCloud. I can't see them.
  • The smart features run on your device using Apple's built-in model.
  • Anonymous usage counts are sent only if you say yes, and you can change that any time.

What Henwood stores, and where

Your pantry, shopping list, recipes, meal plan, notes and chat history are saved on your device. If iCloud is on, Henwood syncs them through your own private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they appear on your other devices. That data is stored by Apple under your Apple Account. I don't run a server and can't see, read or delete it.

If you share your kitchen with someone, the shared items go through iCloud sharing to the people you invite, and they can see and change them. You can stop sharing at any time.

Smart features, Siri and voice

Answers in the chat, reading recipes and scanning photos run on your device with Apple's on-device model. Henwood doesn't send your questions or kitchen to an AI service.

When you speak to Henwood in the app, speech is turned into text on your device. When you use Siri, your request is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and Henwood only gets the request Siri passes to it.

Permissions

Henwood only asks when you use a feature that needs it, and you can turn each one off in Settings.

  • Camera: to scan recipes and receipts. Photos stay on your device.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: to tell Henwood what changed out loud. Speech is processed on your device.
  • Reminders: if you choose, to keep your shopping list in Reminders and mark things bought when you check them off.
  • Calendar: if you choose, to put planned meals in a Meals calendar.

When Henwood goes online

  • Recipes you import: when you share or paste a link, Henwood downloads that page to read the recipe. The website sees a normal visit from your device.
  • Recipe feeds: the Feeds tab downloads new posts from the food sites listed there.
  • iCloud: for sync and sharing, as described above.
  • Usage counts, only if you said yes (below).

Usage counts (optional)

When you first open Henwood, it asks whether you'd like to share anonymous usage counts. Nothing is sent unless you say yes. If you do, Henwood sends counts of which features get used and how often (for example, "a recipe was imported" or "the chat was used"), plus when the app was opened, to Amplitude, an analytics service. This helps me decide what to build next.

These counts never include what's in your kitchen: no item names, recipe titles, chat text, photos or notes. There's no account or name attached. Amplitude uses a random ID that resets if you reinstall the app, and IP address, city and carrier collection is turned off. Henwood doesn't use Apple's advertising identifier. You can turn sharing off any time in Henwood under Settings, Privacy.

Deleting your data

  • Delete items, recipes and chats in the app, and the deletion syncs to your other devices.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from that device.
  • To remove Henwood's iCloud data everywhere, go to Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then iCloud, then Storage, and remove Henwood's data.

Children

Henwood isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If Henwood starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before the change ships, with a new date at the top.
