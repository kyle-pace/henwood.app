Common questions

How do I get my kitchen on my other devices?
Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on. Henwood syncs on its own.

Can someone else share my kitchen?
Yes. Invite them from Henwood's settings. Shared kitchens use iCloud sharing, so they'll need an Apple Account.

Can I get my recipes out?
Yes. Any recipe can be shared as a Henwood file, a PDF or Markdown.

What happens to my data?
It stays on your device and in your iCloud. See the privacy page for details.
