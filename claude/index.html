Henwood

Henwood in Claude

For Henwood on the Mac. This is an experiment, so things may change.

What it does
Claude can read your pantry, shopping list and saved recipes in Henwood, and make changes for you. Ask what you can make tonight, what's on your list, or tell it you're out of eggs. Every change shows up in Henwood's Home chat, where you can undo it.

Set it up

  1. In Henwood on your Mac, open Settings, then Assistants, and turn on Let AI assistants use Henwood.
  2. Choose Copy Token.
  3. Download the extension and open it. Claude asks whether to install Henwood.
    Download Henwood for Claude
  4. When Claude asks for the Henwood token, paste it. Leave the port as it is; the extension finds Henwood on its own.
  5. Start a new chat in Claude and ask away.

Already installed an earlier version? Open the new download to update it. Needs the Claude app for Mac. Henwood has to be open for Claude to reach it; closing its window is fine.

Things to ask

  • "What's on my Henwood shopping list?"
  • "What can I make tonight without going to the store?"
  • "Find a recipe that uses up the spinach."
  • "We're out of eggs and low on rice."
  • "Add what I need for the coconut dal to my list."
  • "Save this recipe to Henwood" with a link.

Other assistants

Claude Code: in Henwood's Settings, choose Copy for Claude Code and paste the command into Terminal.

Anything else that supports MCP (ChatGPT's desktop app, Cursor, VS Code and others): choose Copy Setup for Other Apps and add Henwood as a Streamable HTTP server with that address and header.

Your privacy

  • It's off until you turn it on, and Henwood asks first.
  • Only apps on your Mac can connect, and only with your token. Nothing goes through a Henwood server; there isn't one.
  • What Claude reads from Henwood goes to Claude, and Anthropic handles it under its privacy policy. Other assistants work the same way under their own policies.
  • Turn it off any time in Settings, Assistants. Choosing New Token disconnects everything set up before.

Questions or trouble: hello@henwood.app.
